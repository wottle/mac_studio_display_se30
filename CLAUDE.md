# Mac Studio Display SE/30

GitHub repo: `wottle/mac_studio_display_se30` (renamed 2026-11-19; earlier names NeoMac_LCD_Case and neomac-lcd-case, and the working name "Neo-Mac" survives in file names such as `neomac.py`, `neomac_lcd_case.3mf` and `neomac_assembly.step`; leave those unless Mike asks).

A 3D-printed monitor shell for an iPad 1/2 LCD that looks like a thin, futuristic classic Mac.
Sister project to the Snow White Happy Pi (slim offset case). This one is a **monitor only: no Pi inside**.

Current version: **v0.12** (2026-11-19). This folder is the GitHub repo for the project; Mike commits and pushes it himself.
Mike printed a base at v0.8 (v0.9 changed only its badge recess). v0.10 and v0.11 change only the bay lid; the cover plate is unchanged. v0.12 adds an optional cover plate with a handle; nothing existing changed.

## Design in one paragraph

A 12 mm ribbon. The side profile is the whole idea: a flat face tilted back 8 degrees, a chin that steps **in** under the face (classic Mac chins recede, they do not stick out), a rounded lip, a short riser, and a flat foot running back. SE/30-style contour lines cross the chin. The lowest line hides the seam between the two big parts and carries the floppy slot.

Overall 250 W x 162 D x 333 H mm. Mike's bed is 350 x 350 x 350.

## Decisions and why (do not undo without asking Mike)

- **Three-part shell:** base, front frame, back cover. Mike asked for this split.
- **Seam hidden in the lowest contour line.** The line and the floppy notch are cut in the **base**, just below the seam; the frame's bottom edge is plain. Reason: when the line was a rebate on the frame's bottom edge, that raised lip rounded off on the bed (found on the coupon print).
- **Joint:** short wedge tongue on the base (flat front face, 45 deg rear face) into a groove in the frame, for alignment only. A 3 mm full-width flange on the base laps 17.5 mm up behind the frame, recessed flush into a rebate. Three M3x8 go through the flange into heat-set inserts in the frame. Reason: the earlier version self-tapped into a 4 mm thick tongue with under 1 mm of plastic above the pilot hole; Mike asked for inserts.
- **Tongue clearance 0.2 mm** (`TG_CLR`). Coupon-tested; 0.1 and 0.3 were worse.
- **Mating edges stay square.** No chamfer on the seam, tongue, flange front or flange top, so the joint shows as little as possible.
- **0.6 mm edge break** (`EDGE_CH`) on the base's side-face outline and on the frame's front and rear perimeters (not the bottom). The long side edges that cross the joint use the same 0.6 mm on both parts so they run straight through the seam. The frame's front chamfer was 1.2 mm until v0.8; it was reduced to match.
- **No separate black bezel.** The opening (199.0 x 149.8 mm) sits inside the panel's own black border (200.5 x 151.2 mm) and about 1 mm outside the lit area (197.1 x 147.8 mm). The panel's metal edge is hidden by only 0.7 mm at nominal position, so positioning matters.
- **LCD mounting:** the pocket leaves 4.75 mm past the panel tabs each side, 16.4 mm below and 5.9 mm above nominal. Four flat brackets on recessed seats press the panel's back edge forward (1.0 mm gap for a foam pad). Optional sliding stops set height and X; each comes in three shapes because mounting tabs sit in different places on different panels. Stand-off shapes clear a tab about 4 mm proud.
- **Controller bay:** 21 mm clear above the frame's rear face (`BAY_IN_H`), lid walls at 30 deg from vertical; both changed in v0.10 because Mike wanted it deeper and steeper to print better. The lid's footprint and its four post positions are fixed by the printed cover plate: change depth and angle only, or the plate must be reprinted. No board mounts. Vents (v0.11, Mike's request): 20 stadium slots 3.5 x 12 mm on an 8 mm pitch in the sloping top wall (`VENT_*`), long axis down the slope so in print orientation only the 3.5 mm round ends bridge (the overhang check counts those separately); centres kept 13.5 mm clear of the upper posts. Boards are taped to the back of the LCD. The bay is open at the bottom, and a trough behind the chin deepens the opening to 26 mm over the middle 150 mm so a VGA plug drops straight down.
- **Back cover in two pieces** (plate + lid) so neither needs supports.
- **Ballast is required.** Two crosswise channels at the rear of the foot for 1" x 3/16" bar, same idea as the Happy Pi. Estimated forward tip angle: 2 deg with no bar, 11 with one, 17 with two (assumed masses, see `docs/validation_report.txt`).
- **Badge recess:** for Mike's standard square badge plate; square 12 x 12 mm, 1.6 mm deep, square corners (`BADGE*`), on the base's lower band at the left; centre 29 mm from the left side and 14 mm below the seam.
- **Carry handle (v0.12, Mike's request):** optional alternate cover plate, inspired by the compact Mac's top-rear grip but protruding instead of recessed. A squared ledge, 120 mm wide (Mike chose narrower than the 150 mm mock-up), 30 mm out, 6 mm shelf, lip dropping 7 mm with a 45 deg inner face, cheeks leaning in at `BAY_TAPER`, top at z 231 so the top-centre cover screw stays reachable. Printed **as part of the plate**, inner face down, so it needs no extra holes, inserts or screws (Mike wanted none). Finger gap behind the lip 15 mm at the shelf, 22 mm at the lip bottom; widened from 9 mm after the first section view by thinning the root web to 5 mm. Rejected: hollow hoods and a bridge pull (would have needed a separate screw-on part).
- **Seats are recessed** (`SEAT_Y = 8.0`) so bracket and stop screw heads stay under the cover. Clearance is 0.15 mm with a 1.65 mm button head; taller heads need deeper seats.

## Coordinate systems

- **Body frame** (frame, cover, LCD, stops): +X is the viewer's right when facing the screen, Y = 0 is the front face and +Y goes to the rear, Z = 0 is the seam and +Z is up.
- **World frame:** the body frame tilted back 8 deg and placed on the base stub; the desk is Z = 0. The base is modelled directly in the world frame.
- `place()` moves a body-frame shape into the world frame. `to_world()` does the same for a point.
- "Left" and "right" in part names mean the viewer's left and right when facing the **screen** (left = low X). Working from the rear with the cover off, a "left" part sits on your right.

## Working rules for this project

- Change parameters at the top of `neomac.py` first; most features follow from them.
- After any geometry change run `export_all.py` and do not ship unless it prints `ALL PASS`. Add a check when you add a feature that could collide or leave a thin wall.
- Keep every part support-free in its listed orientation. If a feature cannot be, say so plainly and offer an alternative.
- Insert holes need at least 2.5 mm of plastic between the hole bottom and the front face, and must not land on a contour line.
- Keep joint mating edges square (see above).
- Bump the version, record the change in the version history below, update README.md if anything it states changed, and tell Mike which parts need reprinting (and that the 3MF needs the new STLs).
- Mike prints coupons before big parts. Offer coupons for any new fit.
